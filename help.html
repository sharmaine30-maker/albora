C. Shipping & Delivery

Orders are shipped with J&T Express.

The shipping fee is added after payment verification. The fee amount is not calculated on this Help page.

Delivery timing depends on the destination and courier. Please refer to the order information or contact Alborá for assistance with a specific order.

D. Product Customization

Some products offer choices such as shade, finish, color, size, or gift packaging. Available choices are shown in each product's details before you add it to your bag.

Perfumes offer 10 ml, 30 ml, and 50 ml sizes. A 10 ml box has an additional charge when selected; boxes for 30 ml and 50 ml are included at no additional charge.

Available jewelry choices depend on the piece. Options may include a finish, necklace length, ring size, or a matching jewelry box. The product details show which choices apply.

Gift packaging options are shown for applicable products and may include Alborá gift bags or an exclusive gift box. Flowers do not have gift packaging options.

E. Returns & Refunds

Contact Alborá with your order details and explain what is different from your order. The team can help review the issue.

Use the Contact Us section below to ask for assistance. No return or refund policy is published on this page, so Alborá will need to review your request and advise you directly.
